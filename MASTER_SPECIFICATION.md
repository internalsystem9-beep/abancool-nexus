## 💳 MULTI-TENANT POS SAAS SYSTEM

### Critical Design Principle

**This POS is NOT for one business.**

**This is a MULTI-BUSINESS SaaS POS PLATFORM** where:
- MANY businesses subscribe and use the system independently
- Each business has completely isolated data
